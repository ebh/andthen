# Privacy policy

Version 2 · Effective 2026-11-17

This policy explains what andthen.page ("we", "us") stores about you, why, for how long and who else gets it. You do not have to accept it. It is shown and linked so that you can read it at any time.

Questions or requests: support@andthen.page.

## What we store

We store this in a database on Cloudflare:

- **Your user record.** If you have signed in, it holds your Clerk user ID. If you have not, it holds a SHA-256 hash of the device token that your device keeps. We store only the hash, never the token itself. We also store when the record was made.
- **Your display name.** You choose one display name, the first time you start or join a story. We store it with your user record, so it is the same in every story you are in. Other writers in those stories see it. No two people can use the same display name.
- **Passages.** We store each passage you write, who wrote it and when.
- **Participation times.** We store when you joined a story and when you left it.
- **Suggestion request times.** Each time you ask for AI suggestions, we store the time. We use these to enforce the limit of 3 requests per hour.
- **Push subscriptions.** If you turn on turn notifications, we store the address of your browser's push service (the endpoint) and the encryption keys your browser gave us.

We use this data to run the app: to keep your stories, work out whose turn it is, limit abuse and send the turn notifications you ask for.

## Cookies and device storage

On the web, we set one cookie, `__Host-andthen_bt`. It is HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read it. It lasts 365 days. It carries only your device token, which tells us which anonymous user you are on that browser. If you clear it, we can no longer tell that your stories belong to you, unless you have signed in.

The store apps have no cookies to rely on. They make a device token on first launch and keep it in the secure storage that the phone provides.

We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Our fonts are bundled with the app. None are loaded from a font service.

## Accounts

An account is optional. The app works without one. An account lets you reach your stories from any device.

You sign in by email through Clerk, a service that holds your email address and manages your sign-in. We keep only your Clerk user ID, not your email address. Clerk's scripts load on every page of the web app, and Clerk may set its own cookies. Clerk's own policy covers what it does with your data.

When you sign in on a device that already has stories, those stories and their passages move to your account. If your account already existed, the anonymous user record for that device is merged into it and deleted. The display name of your account is kept. If your account had none, it takes the display name from the device. The passages you wrote on that device then show under your account's display name, and the other writers in those stories see it there.

## AI suggestions

When the turn holder asks for suggestions, we send up to the last 6,000 characters of the story's text to a Llama model run by Cloudflare Workers AI. We send no display names. We do not store the text we send or the suggestions we get back.

Cloudflare states that it does not retain the inputs or outputs of Workers AI requests after they are no longer needed to process the request. It also states that it does not use customer content processed by Workers AI to train AI models, and does not make it available to other Cloudflare customers. These are Cloudflare's statements, not ours.

## Turn notifications

Turn notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. We send them with Web Push, through the push service of your browser. That is Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser.

Each message is encrypted for your browser. It holds the first 60 characters of the story's opening passage and the invite link. It holds no display names. The push service can see that a message was sent to your browser and when. It cannot read the message.

## Hosting and where data goes

We run on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and Durable Objects). Cloudflare processes IP addresses in its own logs for hosting and security. The app itself does not read your IP address or browser details. Our own logs record errors and technical events, not the text of stories.

We set no region for our data, so it is processed outside Australia, in countries where Cloudflare operates. Clerk is a service based in the United States. Your data may therefore be handled there.

## Who can see what

- **Display names and passages** are visible to everyone in the story. Anyone who has the invite link can join it, up to the limit for the story, and then read it.
- **Your display name is the same in every story.** Someone who shares two stories with you can tell that you wrote in both.
- **Anyone can find out whether a display name is in use.** We refuse a display name that someone else already has, in any story, so trying one shows whether it is taken. It does not show who has it or which stories they are in.
- **The invite link is the only secret.** Share it only with people you want in the story.
- **If you leave a story,** your display name stays on the passages you wrote, so the story still makes sense.
- **We can see** everything we store, to run and protect the service. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
- **Push services, Clerk and Cloudflare** get the data described above, and no more.

## How long we keep data

We keep what we store until you ask us to delete it, except in the cases below.

Things we delete or clear without a request are:

- A push subscription is deleted when you switch turn notifications off, when you sign out, or when the push service tells us it no longer exists.
- The anonymous user record for a device is deleted when it merges into your account.
- When you sign in for the first time on a device, the device token hash is removed from your user record.
- When you rejoin a story, the time you left it is cleared.
- A suggestion request time is deleted if the request fails and you got no suggestions, so the failed request does not count against your limit.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it. Email support@andthen.page and tell us which stories are yours. We may ask you to prove it, for example by sending us an invite link and your display name.

We delete the data by hand within 30 days of your request. A way to do this inside the app is planned.

Some of your data sits inside stories that other writers share. When you ask for deletion, we will tell you what we can remove and what stays because other writers rely on it.

## Children

andthen.page is not for anyone under 16. If we learn that we hold data from someone under 16, we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we publish it as a new version with its own number and date. Old versions stay available. The version and effective date are at the top of this page.

## What changed in version 2

Under version 1, you chose a display name for each story. Version 2 gives each person one display name for all their stories. When we made the change, each person kept the display name from the story they joined most recently, and it now shows on all of their passages. Where two people ended up with the same display name, the person whose user record was made first kept it. For a signed-in person, that is when the record their account uses was made, which can be later than when they first used the app. Each other person has the next free number added, such as "Sam 2".

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, contact us first at support@andthen.page. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) under the Australian Privacy Principles.
