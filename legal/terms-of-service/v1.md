Suggestions only fill the text box. Nothing is added to the story until you send it. You can change a suggestion or ignore it. An AI model can make mistakes and can write things that are wrong or offensive.

If you send text that came from a suggestion, you are the writer of that passage. You are responsible for it in the same way as any passage you type yourself.
